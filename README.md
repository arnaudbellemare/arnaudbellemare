# Arnaud Bellemare

**Building practical AI tools for businesses and the people who use them.**

I work at the intersection of AI, software, and business operations. My projects explore AI search visibility, local-first tools, and interfaces that make technical capabilities easier to use. I’m interested in AI product and engineering roles, consulting, and thoughtful collaborations.

## Selected work

### [GEOSITION](https://github.com/arnaudbellemare/geosition) · AI search visibility

A source-available toolkit for auditing websites for AI search readiness. It brings together a Python CLI, a web platform, technical checks, and evidence-oriented reporting. The readiness score is a heuristic—not a guarantee of citations.

[Website](https://www.geositionai.com/) · [Documentation](https://arnaudbellemare.github.io/geosition/)

### [AI History Kit](https://github.com/arnaudbellemare/ai-history-kit) · Local-first AI tooling

A Python toolkit to back up, search, and review AI conversation history locally, with secret redaction and user-reviewed memory candidates.

### [Gestion Velora website](https://github.com/arnaudbellemare/GestionVeloraWebsite) · Business software and web

A business website project that combines a customer-facing experience with analytics and search measurement. [Visit the website](https://gestionvelora.com/).
